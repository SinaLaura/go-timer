# Google Play: store listing

Everything to copy into the Play Console. Limits are Google's.

## English (default language)

**App name** (max 30)

Go Girls* Timer

**Short description** (max 80)

A Go clock for your phone, next to the board. Fischer, byo-yomi, works offline.

**Full description** (max 4000)

A game clock for Go that lives on your phone, lying next to the board.

Tap your half of the screen after your move and your opponent's clock starts. Big numbers, readable from across the board, and the screen stays on while the clock runs.

TIME SETTINGS
• Fischer: a main time, plus seconds after every move (default 45 min + 15 s)
• Byo-yomi: a main time, then periods that start again after every move (default 30 min + 5 × 30 s)
• Simple timer: just a main time

DURING A GAME
• Under 10 seconds your half turns pink and a soft chime sounds
• When time runs out, a voice says who lost, in English or German
• Pause any time to see the statistics, and a chart of the time you spent on every move

MADE TO BE QUIET
• Works offline, once opened
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone.

And a little secret: during a game, tap the heart on the running clock.

A Go Girls* App by Sina. 🖤

## Answers for the Play Console forms

- **Privacy policy:** https://sinalaura.github.io/go-timer/privacy.html
- **App or game:** Game
- **Category:** Board, where the other Go apps are
- **Tags:** pick Board games, Strategy and Tools if they're offered
- **Ads:** No
- **Data safety:** No data collected, no data shared
- **Content rating questionnaire:** answer No to everything. It ends up rated for everyone.
- **Target audience:** 13 and over. Including under-13s brings extra rules for children's apps.
- **Contact email:** Google shows this publicly in the store, so use one you're fine sharing.

## PWABuilder settings

- **Package ID:** `io.github.sinalaura.gotimer`. It can never change later.
- **Notification delegation:** off. The app sends no notifications, and the privacy page says it asks for no permissions.
- **Signing key:** let PWABuilder make a new one, then keep the downloaded key file and its passwords safe forever (cloud drive and a USB stick). Without it you can't update the app.
